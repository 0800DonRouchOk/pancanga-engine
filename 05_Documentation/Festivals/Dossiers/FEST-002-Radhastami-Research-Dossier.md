# FEST-002 Radhastami Research Dossier

Campaign: 62.1
Status: PARTIAL

## Question

Can Radhastami be selected by a generic `masa + paksa + tithi` rule?

## Evidence Located

- Both local operational calendars identify Sri Radhastami as an Astami
  observance. This is Tier 5 validation/discovery evidence only.
- *Acarya Kesari Sri Srimad Bhakti Prajnana Kesava Gosvami: His Life and
  Teachings*, p. 131, states that Hari-bhakti-vilasa refers to the Radhastami
  tithi as a vrata but does not prescribe fasting. This is a Tier 3 lineage
  witness and a lead to a still-unlocated Tier 1 passage.
- The complete HBV reader inspected in this campaign did not yield a searchable
  direct Radhastami passage. Absence from OCR/search is not proof of absence.
