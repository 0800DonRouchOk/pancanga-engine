# FEST-009 Govardhana Puja Research Dossier

Campaign: 62.1
Status: PARTIAL-HIGH EVIDENCE

## Evidence Located

Hari-bhakti-vilasa 16.231-236 supplies a materially richer rule than a simple
tithi lookup:

```text
Karttika, Sukla Pratipada
worship in the morning
Amavasya-Pratipada contact is accepted/preferred for go-puja
Pratipada affected by Dvitiya is rejected for the rite
completion before noon is required
```

The campaign inspected a complete Sanskrit-English reader whose translation
witness is attributed to Bhumipati Dasa and edited by Purnaprajna Dasa. The
download records all twenty vilasas and 9,479 numbered texts. Publication year,
publisher and independent commentary verification remain incomplete.

## Unresolved

- exact computable interpretation of `pratah` and `before noon`;
- whether the Amavasya-contact statement controls all Govardhana worship or a
  narrower go-puja/go-krida component;
- Dig-darsini reading and accepted edition;
- two-sunrise and ksaya-tithi behavior;
- local versus reference-location policy.

## Outcome

Govardhana Puja is not a generic simple-tithi festival. It requires a
`TITHI_WITH_CONTACT_PRIORITY` subclass and a sourced day-part boundary.
