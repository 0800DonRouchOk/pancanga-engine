# FEST-010 Jagannatha Ratha-yatra Research Dossier

Campaign: 62.1
Status: RULE CONFLICT

## Evidence Located

- The official Puri tourism page identifies the annual event with Asadha Sukla
  Dvitiya. This is operational/institutional evidence, not by itself Tier 1.
- A Sri Jagannath Temple Administration response cites Skanda Purana,
  Purusottama-ksetra-mahatmya and Brahma Purana for a nine-day cycle beginning
  on Asadha Sukla Dvitiya and ending with Bahuda.
- The same response preserves an opposing interpretation that additional tithis
  can authorize Ratha-yatra. The underlying editions and passages have not been
  acquired into the project.
- HBV contains Ratha-yatra material, but the inspected Vilasa 16 context does not
  by itself establish that every passage governs the annual Puri Asadha event.
