# FEST-093 Guru/Vyasa Purnima Research Dossier

Campaign: 62.1
Status: PARTIAL / SCOPE UNRESOLVED

## Evidence Located

- Both local operational calendars record Guru or Vyasa Purnima in the Asadha
  full-moon area. Tier 5 only.
- A Kanchi publication describes Vyasa Puja on Asadha Purnima at the beginning
  of Caturmasya. This is a traceable but non-Gaudiya lineage witness.
- A Gaudiya publication on Vyasa-puja records that Bhaktisiddhanta Sarasvati did
  not use the common Guru Purnima custom for his own Vyasa-puja and instead ties
  disciple-led Vyasa-puja to the guru's appearance tithi.
- SCS Math identifies its calendar as derived from *Sri Gaudiya Parva Talika*
  and follows a Kolkata reference date for non-Ekadasi observances.

## Scope Problem

`Guru Purnima`, worship of Vyasa on Asadha Purnima, and an acarya's annual
`Vyasa-puja` cannot be assumed to be interchangeable records.
