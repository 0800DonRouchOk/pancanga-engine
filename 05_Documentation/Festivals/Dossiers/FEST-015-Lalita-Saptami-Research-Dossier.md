# FEST-015 Lalita Saptami Research Dossier

Campaign: 62.1
Status: PARTIAL / IDENTITY AMBIGUITY

## Evidence Located

- Both local operational calendars record Sri Lalita Saptami as the appearance
  observance of Lalita-devi. This is Tier 5 discovery evidence.
- A searchable Sanskrit witness of Bhavisya Purana 2.2.7.40 contains the words
  `saptami lalita bhadre` and a para/trisandhya expression.
- The delivered page identifies S. N. Khandelwal's 2022 Sanskrit-Hindi edition
  as its text source and lists a 2023 Shantilal Nagar English edition as another
  edition.

## Critical Ambiguity

The campaign has not established that this Bhavisya Purana `Lalita` passage
denotes the Gaudiya Lalita-sakhi appearance observance rather than another
Lalita vrata/tradition. Its larger chapter context and translation are absent.

## Unresolved

```text
canonical identity of the cited Lalita
accepted Gaudiya source
meaning of para and trisandhya-vyapini in context
sunrise/two-day selection
fasting and parana
reference-location policy
```

## Outcome

Possible simple-tithi member, but the apparent source cannot be promoted until
identity and context are verified.
