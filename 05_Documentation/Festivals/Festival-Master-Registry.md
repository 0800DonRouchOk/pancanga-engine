## Scope and Method

This registry consolidates names found in the existing Festival Engine documents,
the local PureBhakti 2026 calendar and the local SCS Math 2026-2027 calendar.
Those calendars are discovery and future validation material only. Their entries
do not become normative rules through inclusion here.

The 24 ordinary Ekadasis and 8 Mahadvadasis are already governed by the existing
Observance Catalog. They are linked as a 32-record implemented baseline and are
not duplicated with `FEST-*` IDs.

`canonical_name` is the project's romanized Sanskrit/Bengali label. Unknown
calendar markers are intentionally preserved as `UNKNOWN`.

## Status Vocabulary

```text

LRR  LINEAGE_RESEARCH_REQUIRED
P    PARTIAL local discovery evidence
U    UNVERIFIED / RESEARCH_REQUIRED
```
