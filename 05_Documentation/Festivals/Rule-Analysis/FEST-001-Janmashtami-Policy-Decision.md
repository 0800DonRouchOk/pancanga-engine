## 2. Exact Ambiguity

The ambiguity is not the Saptamī-viddhā case. That case is already resolved:
the contaminated earlier date is rejected even when Rohiṇī is present.

The remaining ambiguity begins only after the resolver has applied the common
purity rules and still has two legitimate dates. The Dig-darśinī to 15.374
discusses two related forms of that situation.

### 2.1 Class A: Tithi-Vṛddhi Across Two Dates

```text
DAY 1

tithi at sunrise:
Pure Kṛṣṇa Aṣṭamī

tithi during niśītha:
Aṣṭamī may be present; the exact fact must be retained

Rohiṇī status:
May extend with Aṣṭamī across Day 1 and into Day 2

viddhā status:
Not Saptamī-viddhā

Jayantī qualification:
YES when Aṣṭamī and Rohiṇī have positive-duration overlap

Navamī / Saptamī association:
No Saptamī contamination; Navamī follows on Day 2

textual rules satisfied:
Pure Aṣṭamī; HBV 15.374 expressly names the earlier date when both Aṣṭamī and
Rohiṇī extend through the two-day case

textual rules violated:
None
```

```text
DAY 2

tithi at sunrise:
Aṣṭamī may still be present by tithi-vṛddhi, or the day may be connected with
Navamī after Aṣṭamī ends

tithi during niśītha:
Depends on the Aṣṭamī end; the exact fact must be retained

Rohiṇī status:
May remain present, end on Day 2, or form the later Navamī-linked conjunction

viddhā status:
Not Saptamī-viddhā

Jayantī qualification:
YES when Aṣṭamī and Rohiṇī overlap; otherwise the later date may still be
favoured only under a documented commentarial practice

Navamī / Saptamī association:
Later / Navamī-connected, never earlier-Saptamī-contaminated

textual rules satisfied:
HBV 15.352-355 praises the Navamī, Rohiṇī, and weekday combinations; the
Dig-darśinī to 15.374 preserves readings and a Vaiṣṇava practice selecting the
later date

textual rules violated:
None under the corresponding recorded reading
```

Both dates remain viable because HBV 15.374 states an earlier-date rule for
its exact base case, while the Dig-darśinī records later-date readings and
concludes that sampradāya practice is decisive.

### 2.2 Class B: Pure Earlier Aṣṭamī Versus Later Navamī-Linked Date

```text
DAY 1

tithi at sunrise:
Pure Kṛṣṇa Aṣṭamī

tithi during niśītha:
May or may not be Aṣṭamī

Rohiṇī status:
The decisive first-reading distinction is whether Rohiṇī is present at the
earlier ardha-rātra / niśītha

viddhā status:
Not Saptamī-viddhā

Jayantī qualification:
Possible

Navamī / Saptamī association:
No Saptamī contamination

textual rules satisfied:
Pure Aṣṭamī and the base annual vrata

textual rules violated:
None
```

```text
DAY 2

tithi at sunrise:
Navamī, connected to the preceding Aṣṭamī event

tithi during niśītha:
Normally no longer Aṣṭamī; the precise instant remains evidence

Rohiṇī status:
May occur with the later Navamī-linked date

viddhā status:
Not Saptamī-viddhā

Jayantī qualification:
Only if Aṣṭamī and Rohiṇī actually overlap; Rohiṇī with Navamī is a praised
later-date fact but is not silently converted into Aṣṭamī-Rohiṇī overlap

Navamī / Saptamī association:
Navamī-connected

textual rules satisfied:
HBV 15.352-355 and the first Dig-darśinī reading to 15.374 can favour this date
when the earlier date lacks Rohiṇī at ardha-rātra and the later date has the
stated Rohiṇī / Navamī combination

textual rules violated:
None under that reading
```

The two dates are not equivalent astronomical states. They are competing
ritual candidates authorized by different priority readings preserved in the
commentary.

### 3.1 Non-Edges

The following facts are not universal date-selection edges:

```text
Rohiṇī presence alone
Jayantī qualification alone
niśītha overlap alone
Monday alone
Wednesday alone
Navamī association alone
```

They become decisive only inside a source-backed profile that states how the
facts are combined. Treating any one of them as a universal tie-breaker would
be an undocumented implementation policy.

## 7. Candidate Project Policies

The following policies are discrete and implementable. They share the common
purity filter and differ only after more than one legitimate candidate
survives.

### OPTION A: `JAN_TWO_DAY_DDT_PRIMARY`

Classification:

```text
PROJECT_NORMATIVE_POLICY selecting a COMMENTARIAL_PRIORITY
```

Decision:

```text
if Day 1 is Saptamī-viddhā:
    select the eligible later date

else if Day 1 is pure Aṣṭamī and both Aṣṭamī and Rohiṇī are present at
        niśītha:
    select Day 1

else if Day 1 is pure but lacks Rohiṇī at niśītha
     and Day 2 has the documented Navamī-linked Rohiṇī conjunction:
    select Day 2

else:
    select Day 1
```

The final `else` is a `DERIVED_LOGICAL_PRIORITY`, not an explicit universal
sentence in HBV. Selecting Option A would constitute Product Owner approval of
that derived fallback as part of the project policy.

Basis:

```text
HBV 15.357-374
First Dig-darśinī reading to 15.374
```

Consequence:

```text
Preserves the earlier pure date unless the first DDT reading supplies the
specific later Rohiṇī / Navamī priority.
```

Confidence:

```text
HIGH for the explicit branches
MEDIUM for the derived earlier fallback outside the exact stated examples
```

### OPTION B: `JAN_TWO_DAY_DDT_WEEKDAY_OVERRIDE`

Classification:

```text
PROJECT_NORMATIVE_POLICY selecting an alternate COMMENTARIAL_PRIORITY
```

Decision:

```text
if Day 1 is Saptamī-viddhā:
    select the eligible later date

else if Day 2 is Monday or Wednesday and also satisfies the later
        Rohiṇī / Navamī-linked conjunction represented by HBV 15.352-354 and
        the alternate Dig-darśinī reading:
    select Day 2, even over a complete pure Day 1 with Rohiṇī at niśītha

else:
    select Day 1
```

Basis:

```text
The `yadvā` reading in the Dig-darśinī to HBV 15.374
The explicit fallback `anyathā tu pūrvaiva`
```

Consequence:

```text
Makes the later weekday combination decisive and otherwise preserves the
earlier date.
```

Confidence:

```text
HIGH for existence of the alternate reading
MEDIUM until a lineage confirms the exact conjunction predicate it follows
```

### OPTION C: `JAN_TWO_DAY_LATER_VAISNAVA`

Classification:

```text
PROJECT_NORMATIVE_POLICY selecting a recorded Vaiṣṇava practice
```

Decision:

```text
if Day 1 is Saptamī-viddhā:
    select the eligible later date

else if two clean candidates survive:
    select Day 2
```

Basis:

```text
The `kecic ca vaiṣṇavāḥ` position recorded in the Dig-darśinī to HBV 15.374
```

Consequence:

```text
Always selects the later date in the residual clean dual-day case, even when
the additional Rohiṇī conjunction is absent.
```

Confidence:

```text
HIGH that the practice is textually recorded
LOW for attribution to Nityānanda Vaṁśa because no local lineage source exists
```
