## Stop Conditions

Research must stop at `UNRESOLVED` when:

```text
- two accepted authorities can change the selected civil date;
- the source identity is bibliographically unverified;
- a calendar practice is the only evidence;
- a lineage-specific rule lacks an approved profile authority;
- the rule needs an astronomical input not yet characterized.
```
