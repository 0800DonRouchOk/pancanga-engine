Tier 4 - Translation and technical reading witness

The Vilāsa 13 translation is titled *Ekādaśī: The Day of Lord Hari* and dated
Helsingfors, 18 February 2001. It states that it is based on the Haridāsa
Śāstrī edition. The local artifact does not supply a complete modern publisher
or imprint and notes that some astrological Sanskrit material is omitted or
summarized.

Therefore, no new critical date-changing rule may be approved from the English
translation alone. Such a rule requires review against the mūla and relevant
Dig-darśinī passage.
